### 🧭 About me

I design and build production systems end-to-end — from requirements and architecture through implementation, deployment, and optimization. Most of my work is client-facing, which means I don't just write code: I regularly bridge engineering with product strategy, UX, infrastructure, SEO, and brand.

I'm less attached to a specific framework and more focused on **maintainable, commercially-sound systems that solve real business problems.**

- 🔭 Currently architecting **Imara** — an AI-first business operating system for African SMEs (product design, modular services, AI roadmap → MVP)
- 🌍 Long-term, I'm moving from client delivery toward **building products that solve infrastructure and operational challenges for African businesses**
- 🌱 Currently deepening my **React/Next.js**, containerized workflows, and scalable SaaS architecture
- 🤝 Open to collaborating with engineers who value pragmatic architecture, rapid iteration, and shipping over unnecessary complexity
