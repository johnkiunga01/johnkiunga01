### About

- 🧭 **Product engineer, not just a coder.** I take products through idea, architecture, design, build, deploy and iterate, and I own the result. That has meant 50+ live launches for 200+ clients in Kenya and abroad.
- 🌍 **Building for Africa.** My work is shaped by local realities: mobile-first users, M-Pesa payments, patchy connectivity and lean teams.
- 🤖 **AI where it pays.** I use LLMs and automation to remove real operational work, not to put a chatbot on top of it.
- 🎨 **Design-literate.** I work in Figma and the Adobe suite, so the interfaces I ship look the way they were designed.

### 🚀 Now building: [Imara](https://imaraapp.com)

An **AI-first business operating system for African SMEs**. CRM, sales, payments, workflow automation and business intelligence live in one product, with AI that turns a small business's data into its next action.

Right now I'm working on product architecture, the design system, and AI integration across the sales and customer workflows.
